   Company Leave Policy

   Section 1: Annual Leave
   Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

   Section 2: Sick Leave
   Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive days.

   Section 3: Requesting Leave
   All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance.
